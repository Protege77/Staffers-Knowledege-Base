## Summary

The U.S. military faces a cost-asymmetry problem where inexpensive drones are intercepted using significantly more expensive air-defense missiles, creating substantial budgetary challenges. Military officials are developing alternative defense solutions to address this disparity and improve cost-effective strategic capabilities.
