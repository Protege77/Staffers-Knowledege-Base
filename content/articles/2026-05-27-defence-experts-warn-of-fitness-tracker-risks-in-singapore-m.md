## Summary

Defence experts in Singapore have warned that fitness tracking apps like Strava could expose the daily routines and movement patterns of military personnel, following high-profile breaches overseas involving French and British military forces. Military installations in Singapore including Sungei Gedong Camp, Changi Naval Base, and Sembawang Air Base have been found on Strava's global heatmap, raising security concerns about operational security.
