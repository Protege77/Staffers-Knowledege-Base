## Summary

RAM chip prices have surged dramatically, with some retailers reporting unprecedented price increases of 200-250% in recent months, causing customers to delay computer purchases and upgrades. Sim Lim Square retailers are experiencing significant losses as the memory chip market faces one of its highest price spikes in decades.
