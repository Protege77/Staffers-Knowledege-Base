## Summary

The U.S. Army is developing new tactics and doctrine to combat drone swarms through exercises like Project Flytrap, which tested counter-UAS defenses and offensive drone strategies with nearly 1,000 personnel in Lithuania. Soldiers are being trained to detect threats from multiple altitudes, including learning to identify drone sounds, while the Army establishes standardized testing frameworks and leverages additive manufacturing to rapidly adapt systems in the field.
