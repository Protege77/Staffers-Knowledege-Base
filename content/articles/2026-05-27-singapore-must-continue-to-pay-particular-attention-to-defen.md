## Summary

Prime Minister Lawrence Wong emphasized that Singapore must prioritize defence spending as modern battlefields evolve with drone technology, noting the challenge of defending against cheap swarms of drones using expensive assets. Wong warned that if the global order deteriorates, small countries like Singapore would face increased vulnerability and unpredictability in a world where nations may resort to force and coercion.
