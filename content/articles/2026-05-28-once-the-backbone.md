## Summary

Command Sergeant Major Nancy Weinberger argues that the noncommissioned officer corps, traditionally the backbone of the U.S. Army, has experienced erosion in customs, discipline, and professional standards over recent decades. She calls for senior NCOs to recommit to their foundational role by actively preserving military traditions, enforcing discipline, and restoring the cultural and professional standards that have historically defined the corps.
