## Summary

The US military conducted strikes on an Iranian military site in Bandar Abbas while Iran's IRGC claimed to have targeted a US air base, escalating tensions amid a fragile ceasefire. The renewed hostilities include US defensive actions against Iranian drone and missile threats, with both sides accusing each other of ceasefire violations and threatening further retaliation.
