## Summary

A US bombing of an Iranian school was widely attributed to AI chatbots like Claude, but the actual cause was a failure to update military databases and the speed of the Maven targeting system built by Palantir Technologies. The article argues that public obsession with AI distracted from the systemic failures and human decision-making that led to the deaths of nearly 180 children.
